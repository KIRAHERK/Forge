## Forge 1.12.0

- **Forge speaks Arabic.** The whole app now comes in Arabic, laid out right to left, with Changa and Cairo fonts. Buttons and labels are in simple Arabic; the motivation lines are in casual Syrian. Numbers stay Western (117, not ١١٧).
- It follows your phone's language. To choose yourself: **Settings → Language** (phone's language, English or العربية), or on the first setup screen.
- Exercise names are in Arabic too, the way people say them in the gym. The step-by-step exercise guides, recipes and the basic food list are still in English for now.

Your data is untouched.
