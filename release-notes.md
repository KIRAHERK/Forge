## Forge 1.7.0

- **Sick days.** Too sick to train? Tap **I'm sick today** under the workout card. The workout is skipped with no penalty and no XP lost, and your streak pauses instead of resetting.
  - Forgot to mark it yesterday? Use **I was sick yesterday** on the make-up card. It cancels yesterday's penalty and gives the XP back.
  - Only today or yesterday can be marked, up to 3 sick days in any 30.
  - Changed your mind? **Undo** on the sick-day card.
  - No workout reminders on a sick day. It shows as a sick icon in the Train week strip and the Habits grid, and doesn't count against your week score.

Your data is untouched.
