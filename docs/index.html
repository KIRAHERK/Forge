A training and nutrition app for Android, in English and Arabic, that holds you to your plan. Pick a program or build your own, log every set, track food and weight, and share your progress.

On a computer? Scan with your phone.

Install in three steps

1

Tap Download for Android on your phone. Forge isn't on the Play Store, so it downloads as a file.

2

Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. It only asks once.

3

Done. Forge checks for updates itself and installs them over the old version, so your data stays.

Inside

Your color

Green Red Purple Pink Blue

Pick one when you set up. It runs through the whole app, the home-screen widget and the pictures you share. Change it any time in Settings.

In Arabic too

The whole app in clear Arabic, laid out right to left. Exercise names come with the English name underneath, and the motivation lines are written in Arabic. It follows your phone's language, or switch in Settings.

التطبيق كاملًا باللغة العربية، من اليمين إلى اليسار.

Programs

Six ready-made programs or build your own from 85 gym and home exercises. Share a program with a QR code.

Every set

Tick sets, see last time's numbers, get told when to add weight. Personal records are spotted for you.

Food

Calories and protein, food search, barcode and label scanning, and 35 Syrian dishes with sourced values. Go over your calories and it tells you.

Consequences

Miss a workout and a penalty lands. Sick? Mark it and nothing is lost. Your data stays on your phone.

Gyms and coaches

Want Forge with your brand and your own programs for your clients? Get in touch.

Share

Turn your progress, a day of eating or a workout into a clean image for Instagram or WhatsApp, in your color.

A guide on first open

A short tour of every tab, with the one thing not to miss on each. Replay it any time with the ? button.

Streaks

Your session streak sits at the top of Today. Habits, protein and weigh-ins have their own streaks and a weekly score.
