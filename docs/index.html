One rule. Missing a session costs you.

Every training day has a deadline. Miss it and the streak breaks, you lose XP, and a real penalty lands that you have to do.

Optional penalty jar: an amount of money you owe yourself for every miss.

Miss the deadline
The streak resets and a penalty lands, like 100 push-ups, due within 24 hours.
Ignore the penalty
It doubles. Ignore it again and it's recorded as failed.
Make it up
Do the session within 48 hours and the streak comes back. The penalty still stands.
Actually sick
Mark a sick day: no penalty, nothing lost. The streak pauses instead of breaking. Up to 3 in any 30 days.

Every set, logged.

Pick a ready-made program or build your own, then tick your sets one by one.

  • Six ready-made programs or your own, from 85 gym and home exercises.
  • Last time's numbers in front of you, a rest timer, and a nudge when to add weight.
  • Personal records spotted for you. Share any program as a QR code.

Food, with the right numbers.

Calories and protein against your targets, from a food list, 35 Syrian dishes with sourced values, or straight from the label.

  • Live label scanning: the four values lock in one by one, and it checks the calories add up.
  • Barcodes, and Syrian products are remembered after one scan. Drinks in ml.
  • 15 budget high-protein recipes and full-day meal plans.

Progress you can see.

Your 7-day average weight, kilos per week against your target, and training volume over eight weeks.

  • Habits you choose, with streaks and a weekly score.
  • Progress photos and measurements, kept privately on your phone.
  • A ready-to-share image of your progress, your day or a workout, for Instagram or WhatsApp.

In your color.

Pick one when you set up. It runs through the whole app, the home-screen widget and the pictures you share. Change it any time.

In Arabic, all of it.

The whole app in clear Modern Standard Arabic, right to left. Exercise names come with the English name underneath, and the motivation lines were written in Arabic, not translated. It follows your phone's language, or switch in Settings.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Nutrition labels are read on the phone; no photo is ever uploaded.

Updates itself

It finds new versions and installs them over the old one with the same key, and backs up before every update.

Install in a minute.

Forge isn't on the Play Store, so it downloads as a file. Here's how:

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. It only asks once.
  3. Done. Forge looks for updates itself, and your data stays.

Play Protect says the app is unknown? That's because it isn't from the Play Store. Tap More details, then Install anyway. Every release is built from the public source on GitHub and signed with the same Forge key.
