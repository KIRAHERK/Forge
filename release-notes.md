## Forge 1.14.0

- **Injury pause.** Injured? Tap **Injured? Pause training** under today's workout. Until you tap **I'm back**, workouts have no deadline, no reminders and no penalties, and your streak is frozen instead of broken. There's no day limit, and it doesn't use up your 3 sick days.
- While paused, food, protein, weigh-ins and your habits keep counting, because they matter most while you heal. Feel up to a session? **Train anyway** logs it as normal.
- Ending the pause keeps today paused too, so tapping **I'm back** late in the day can't cost you a session.

Your data is untouched.
