## Forge 1.2.0

Habits are yours now.

- The Habits tab starts empty. Tap **+** to add any habit, as one to **build** (Done / Not done) or one to **quit** (Clean / Slipped). A quit habit can also count how many times you slipped.
- Each habit gets its own streak and best run, a row in the weekly grid, and a row in Today's check-in. Tap a habit to rename or delete it.
- The urge timer works with any quit habit.
- First-run setup lets you add habits straight away, or skip it.
- Updating from 1.1.x keeps your history: the two habits you tracked become **Habit 1** and **Habit 2**, with every day, streak, slip and XP carried over. Tap each one to give it a name.
